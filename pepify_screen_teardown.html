Pepify — Screen Catalog & UX Teardown

A walkthrough of every screen in the Pepify platform (pepify.pro), documented from a logged-in brand account ("Ascend BioLab"). Pepify is a white-label dropshipping / fulfillment platform: a brand owner signs up, connects a store, funds a prepaid wallet, and Pepify fulfils orders under the brand's own label. This document captures the layout, components, states, and data of each screen so the structure can be rebuilt for a different industry.

How to read this. Each screen entry lists its purpose, layout (top-to-bottom regions), components & controls, states, and data shown. Simplified HTML/CSS "mock" blocks recreate the visual layout of the most important screens so you have a reference without raw screenshots. A companion file, pepify_build_spec.md, contains the buildable blueprint (data model, flows, integrations, MVP phases).

💡 Product & business model

Understanding the model makes every screen self-explanatory — the whole app is built around one money loop.

The core loop
  • Brand owner signs up & subscribes to "Pro" ($97/mo).
  • Connects their own storefront (WooCommerce / Wix) or submits orders manually.
  • Pre-funds a prepaid wallet by credit card (via Stripe).
  • Customer buys on the brand's store & pays the brand directly.
  • Pepify fulfils: picks, applies the brand's white-label vial label, packs, ships.
  • Wholesale cost + flat shipping is auto-deducted from the wallet.
  • The spread (retail − wholesale − shipping − membership) is the brand's profit.
Revenue & pricing levers
  • $97/mo membership (anchored as "raising to $200 soon").
  • Wholesale margin baked into product "Your Cost" vs "Suggested Retail".
  • $12.95 flat shipping per order (USPS Ground Advantage).
  • Wallet deposits are non-refundable (float / lock-in).
  • Referral program: $50 to referrer + $50 to referee on upgrade.
Domain note (peptides & "research only"). Pepify operates in the research-chemical / peptide niche and leans heavily on compliance framing ("for laboratory research only", "≥98% purity", third-party COAs). If you adapt this platform to another industry, the structure transfers cleanly — the compliance/trust layer (COAs, disclaimers, "research use") is the part that's domain-specific and would be swapped for your industry's equivalent trust signals (certificates, authenticity, warranties, etc.).

🎨 Design system

Two themes: a light theme for public/auth pages and a dark theme for the app (default). The aesthetic is a modern fintech/SaaS dashboard — rounded geometric sans, soft cards, generous spacing, a single saturated blue accent, with green/amber/red reserved for status.

Typography

Rounded geometric sans (Poppins/Quicksand-like) for headings; high-contrast white-on-near-black. Bold weights for stat values & CTAs.

Shape & depth

~12–16px corner radii on cards, fully-rounded (pill) buttons & filter tabs, 1px hairline borders, subtle elevation — no heavy shadows.

Motion & feedback

Slide-over drawers from the right, fade-in page transitions, live status dots (green "Online/Live"), toast-style "tips" banner.

🌐 Public funnel — Landing page

/ light thememarketing

A long single-scroll marketing page. Doubles as both "How it Works" and "Catalog" (those nav links are anchor jumps, not separate pages). This is the conversion engine — it sells the $97/mo plan.

Sections, top to bottom
  • Sticky nav — logo · How it Works · Catalog · Sign In · Get Started. Urgency strip: "Closing soon — almost full".
  • Hero — "Launch Your Own Research Peptide Brand In 24 Hours" + dual CTA (Get Started / See Wholesale Pricing) + trust chips (3 US warehouses, 1–2 day fulfilment, Janoshik-verified COAs).
  • How it works — 4 numbered steps (Create account → Activate Pro $97/mo → Set up dashboard → You sell, we fulfil) + "100% Automated" feature column.
  • Research Catalog carousel — product cards showing wholesale cost / suggested retail / profit-per-vial / margin %, with "Out of Stock" badges.
  • Wallet & Payments — 4-card explainer (fund, holds balance, auto-deduct, refunds stay in wallet) + secure-payments note.
  • Interactive Profit Calculator — pick product, set retail, set monthly volume → live profit/order, margin, net monthly profit, break-even.
  • Pricing — single "Pepify Pro" plan, $97/mo, long feature checklist, "price raising to $200" anchor.
  • Testimonials — "4.9/5 from 200+ operators", 6 verified quote cards w/ avatars.
  • FAQ — accordion grouped into How It Works / Your Store & Selling / What's Included / Product Quality.
  • Footer — platform/account/legal columns, disclaimers, cookie consent (Accept / Decline).
Build note
Nearly all marketing copy is reused from in-app explainers (wallet, fulfilment steps, calculator). Treat marketing + app as sharing one content source so they stay consistent.
